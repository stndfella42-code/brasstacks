How it works

Three layers. One business
that runs itself.

Most businesses buy technology in pieces that never talk to each other. We build three layers designed as one system. Scroll, and watch them fall into place.

Layer 01

The Face Your website

Custom-built, no templates. What customers find, and what convinces them.

  • Designed from scratch around your business
  • Online booking, menus, pricing, real content
Layer 02

The Backbone Your operating system

Ordering, inventory, scheduling. One source of truth everything else reads from.

  • POS, bookings, and business data in one place
  • Update it once, and the website and Ava just know
Layer 03

The Voice Ava

One receptionist on every channel, trained on your business.

  • Answers every call 24/7
  • Books, qualifies, and knows your prices cold
  • Chats on your site. Same brain, same memory.
  • Texts you a summary the second each call ends

Update once. Everything knows.

Change a price, add a menu item, shift your hours. You do it once in the backbone. The website shows it. Ava quotes it on the next call. No double entry, no stale pages, no "let me check and call you back."
