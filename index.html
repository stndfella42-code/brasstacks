Why custom

Off-the-shelf almost fits.
Almost isn't good enough.

Most small business owners end up with tech that was built for someone else. You spend more time fighting the tools than running the business. Here's what we refuse to ship:

01

Templates look like everyone else

The shop three doors down is running the same theme. Customers can feel the sameness even when they can't name it. Your business has a personality. Your site should too.

02

Platforms take a cut forever

Monthly fees, transaction percentages, feature paywalls. You rent your own website and never own a byte of it. We build things you own outright, hosted wherever you want.

03

Generic tools fight your workflow

A food truck doesn't operate like a nail salon, and a solo trainer doesn't run like a restaurant. Software built for everyone fits no one. We build around how you actually work.

Brass Tacks exists for one reason: tech that works for you, not the other way around.
