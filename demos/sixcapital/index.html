Why "Six Capital"

A name with history.

From 1836 to 1839, the Republic of Texas moved its capital six times — from Washington-on-the-Brazos to Harrisburg, Galveston, Velasco, Columbia, Houston, and finally Austin. We named the brewery to honor that restless, scrappy spirit. Turns out Aurora sits right on the old Republic's border. Feels like home.
