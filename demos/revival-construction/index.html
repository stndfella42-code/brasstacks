Storm & hail damage

Hail
damage?

We document everything and work directly with your insurance adjuster.

  1. Free roof inspection

    We get up on the roof and check every slope, plus gutters, vents, flashing, siding, and window wraps. Hail damage hides in places you can't see from the driveway.

  2. Photo documentation

    Every bruise, crack, and bald spot gets photographed and marked. You get a clear report you can hand straight to your insurance company.

  3. We meet your adjuster

    We're on the roof when your adjuster inspects, so damage gets counted instead of missed. If something's left off the estimate, we help you ask for a supplement.

  4. Rebuild, then clean up

    Once the claim is approved, we tear off to the deck, replace what's needed, magnet-sweep the yard, and haul everything away.

When does hail hurt a roof?

Asphalt shingles usually start taking real damage around one inch, about the size of a quarter. Wind-driven hail can do it smaller.

Signs to check after a storm

  • Dents in gutters, downspouts, and roof vents
  • Granules washing out of the downspouts
  • Dark, bald spots where granules got knocked off
  • Cracked or split shingles and torn flashing
  • Dinged fins on your AC condenser
