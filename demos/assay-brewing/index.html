    What's on

    Something
    every night.

    No cover, ever. Trivia fills up by 6:45 on Tuesdays, and our live music leans toward bluegrass and outlaw country, loud enough to feel and quiet enough to talk over.

    Want to book the back room for a party of 15 to 60? Email events@assaybrewing.co

    The kitchen

    Food that
    earns a pint.

    Our own kitchen, not a food truck lottery. Pretzels are baked in-house every morning and the green chile is roasted from Pueblo peppers every fall. Kitchen closes an hour before the bar.
