Die Geschichte

Five Centuries in a Star

In the Oberpfalz region of Bavaria, brewing was never a factory job — it was a neighborhood ritual. Families brewed together in the communal brewhouse, then served the fresh beer from their own front rooms.

How did you know whose turn it was? The Zoiglstern — a six-pointed brewer’s star — hung outside the door. When the star was up, the beer was on. No star, no beer.

ZoiglBräu carries that spirit to Aurora: traditional German styles, brewed slowly, served fresh, in a room that feels like the front parlor it is. Tradition in excellence isn’t a slogan — it’s the recipe.
